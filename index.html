Education Major📚

I am a committed, caring person passionate about education, child development, and creating inclusive learning environments. My objective is to work in education, specifically as a kindergarten teacher, with a focus on helping youngsters with a variety of learning needs and disabilities. Every kid should, in my opinion, have the chance to develop, learn, and express themselves in a setting where they are respected, understood, and inspired.

I have gained a deep understanding of the value of patience, empathy, communication, and flexibility when working with children as a result of my school experiences. Through my participation in West Campus High School's Best Buddies program, I was able to develop deep relationships with special education students, promote social interaction, and contribute to the creation of chances for more inclusion within the school community. I discovered the value of creating connections, boosting self-esteem, and making sure every kid feels like they belong by planning events and assisting students in attending school functions like pride rallies.

Working with kids on the autism spectrum and pupils who might need extra educational or developmental support is something that really interests me. I acknowledge that each child has distinct abilities, difficulties, and perspectives on the world. Instead of taking a one-size-fits-all approach to education, I think it's important to accommodate different learning styles, promote personal development, and recognize each student's achievements, no matter how minor. In whatever setting I engage in, I try to maintain an optimistic, encouraging, and receptive mindset. I value working with teachers, families, and other students because I know understanding and teamwork are essential to creating a positive learning environment. Additionally, I am excited to keep learning more about inclusive teaching methods, classroom management, child development, and educational practices.
